About Alexa

The founder your audience actually remembers

Alexa Bigwarfe is an author of more than a dozen books, a military veteran, an adjunct professor of entrepreneurship at the University of South Carolina, and the founder of four companies, including Write | Publish | Sell and Kat Biggie Press.

A dedicated champion of women, she created the Women in Publishing Summit, the first and largest virtual community to empower, encourage, and support women authors and publishing professionals. She hosts multiple podcasts, including the forthcoming Betting on WE.

She is also raising three teenagers, and after a life-threatening illness she embraced the YOLO Life: traveling as much as she can, and documenting the whole thing on Instagram.

On stage, that all shows up as one thing your audience can use: honest strategy, real numbers, and a reason to bet on themselves before they leave the room.
